# Project Title: Indian Sign Language Translation Using Deep Learning Techniques
Description: Indian Sign Language (ISL) is the primary mode of communication for approximately 5 million deaf individuals in India. Despite its importance, ISL faces several challenges that hinder effective communication and technological integration. These include a lack of standardization across regions, limited availability of annotated datasets, insufficient documentation, and a general scarcity of digital tools for ISL development and research. Moreover, public awareness and understanding of ISL remain low, contributing to a persistent communication barrier between the deaf community and the hearing population.
Our project addresses some of these limitations by contributing to the development of digital ISL resources. We utilized datasets from the Indian Sign Language Research and Training Centre (ISLRTC) and selectively curated supplementary data from platforms like Kaggle. Our system focuses exclusively on Indian Sign Language and comprises three key modules:
# Text-to-Sign Conversion – translating written text into ISL.
# Speech-to-Sign Conversion – converting spoken language to ISL.
# Image-to-Sign Conversion – visually representing input information in ISL.
By focusing on the direction of communication from hearing individuals to the deaf community, our solution aims to improve accessibility, raise ISL visibility, and serve as a foundation for future ISL-based innovations.
